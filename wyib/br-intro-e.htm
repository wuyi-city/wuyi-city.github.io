Brief Introduction

Wuyi Institute of Biology is a comprehensive biological research institute affiliated with Jianghe Academy of Sciences. Its predecessor was the Wuyi Prefectural Biological Resources Survey Group founded in 1958. The present institute was formally established in 1986.

The Institute carries out basic and applied research related to biological resources, environmental health and local agricultural and industrial development. Its principal fields include environmental microbiology and public health examination, enzymology and protein biochemistry, molecular genetics of biological resources, and neurophysiology.

The Institute has more than eighty staff members, including over sixty scientific and technical personnel. Four laboratories are open to the public: the Laboratory of Microbiology, the Laboratory of Biochemistry, the Laboratory of Molecular Genetics and the Laboratory of Neurophysiology. Common facilities include an instrument room, an experimental animal facility and a library.

The Institute maintains academic and technical cooperation with universities, research institutions and local organizations in Jianghe Province.
